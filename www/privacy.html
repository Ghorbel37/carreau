Privacy policy

Carreau · last updated 16 October 2026

The short version

Carreau collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything about you anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, music, vibration), best scores, stats, achievements and daily-challenge streak are saved in the app's local storage on your device so they are there next time you play. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Permissions

  • Vibration, for the haptic feedback when you swipe and clear a wall. You can switch it off in the game.

Carreau does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

Carreau is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
