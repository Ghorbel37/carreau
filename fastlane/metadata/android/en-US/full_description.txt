Walls are flying at you, each with holes in a different shape. Swipe to reshape the four corners of your board and slip through before the wall hits.

Carreau started as a notebook sketch: four pieces in the corners of a square, and every swipe stretches or shrinks the two pieces on that side. Simple to learn, fast to play, and hard to master once the walls speed up.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe along a side to resize the two corners on it.
• Fit the shape before the wall arrives. Pieces glow green when they fit their hole.
• Clear walls early and without a wasted swipe for more points. Keep a combo going for up to ×10.

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Gates: walls fly at you faster and faster. Three lives.
• Daily: the same walls for everyone each day. One counted run, keep your streak alive.
• Precision: slower walls, but every wasted swipe costs a life.
• Rush: match as many shapes as you can in 60 seconds.
• Zen: no clock, just shapes.

SPECIAL WALLS AND POWER-UPS
• Blink walls close their holes halfway. Fit them from memory.
• Locked walls chain one side.
• Gold walls are faster and worth triple points.
• Double walls send the next one right behind.
• Collect slow motion, shields and skips along the way.

THREE WORLDS TO UNLOCK
Start on the Triangle, unlock the original square board, Carreau, then take on Ruche, the six-sided hive.
